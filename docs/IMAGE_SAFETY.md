# Image safety


PNG, JPEG and still WebP attachments are accepted up to 20,000,000 bytes each and 20,000,000 bytes combined (up to three). Pillow decodes and re-encodes images without metadata, with a 40 megapixel and 16,000 pixel per-axis limit. JPEG is re-encoded at quality 95; output exceeding the byte limit is rejected. The Bridge accepts up to 27,000,000 bytes of upload JSON including Base64. OpenAI images are individually checked with omni-moderation-latest before generation; flagged or failed checks stop generation. Other providers have no equivalent moderation in Bridge. Do not submit known or suspected CSAM to general moderation; independent child-safety screening and reporting remain release blockers. Visible personal information is not automatically removed. Images are held in transient memory; provider retention and image-token charges apply. Large Studio uploads, live API, native Windows and EXE build remain unverified. No chunked upload implemented. Reference: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/moderation.
